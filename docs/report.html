Flags

COST_NEGATIVE
Net yield is below zero: the costs are larger than what the pool pays.
REWARD_DEPENDENT
More than half of the APY is paid in reward tokens.
APY_OUTLIER
DeFiLlama marks the APY as an outlier, or it is over 3x its 30-day mean.
LOW_TVL
Less than $100,000 is deposited in the pool.
IL_RISK
DeFiLlama says the pool carries impermanent loss risk.
IL_UNKNOWN
Impermanent loss risk, but no 7-day figure exists, so no IL cost is counted.

Assumptions

Position size
$1,000
Holding period
30 days
Reward haircut
50% of the reward APY is assumed lost
Yield
simple, not compounded; the APY is assumed to stay constant
Transactions
2 to enter, 1 to exit, and 1 claim per started 30 days when the pool pays rewards
Swaps
0.30% each way (swap fee + slippage); none for single-asset stablecoin pools
Impermanent loss
7-day IL scaled to the holding period and capped at 50% of the position; not counted when DeFiLlama gives no figure
TVL floor
pools under $100,000 are left out

Notes

  • Offline run: no network calls were made. Gas prices and native token prices are the defaults from the cost table.

Limitations

  • These numbers come from a simple model. They are estimates, not predictions, and not financial advice.
  • The pool data comes from DeFiLlama and is only as good as what protocols and DeFiLlama report. APYs change every day.
  • Impermanent loss is only counted when DeFiLlama gives a 7-day figure (il7d). That field was empty for every pool on these chains when this tool was written, so pools with IL risk are flagged IL_UNKNOWN and their net APY is optimistic.
  • Gas and L1 data fees are rough estimates. Swap cost is a flat rate, not a price-impact model.
  • The reward haircut is a rule of thumb, not a forecast of any token's price.
  • Smart contract risk, depegs, taxes, bridge costs and withdrawal limits are not modelled.
