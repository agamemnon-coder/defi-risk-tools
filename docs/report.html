LOW_TVL
Less than $100,000 is deposited in the pool.
IL_RISK
DeFiLlama says the pool carries impermanent loss risk.

IL_UNKNOWN
Impermanent loss risk, but no 7-day figure exists, so no IL cost is counted.

Assumptions

Position size
$1,000
Holding period
30 days
Reward haircut
50% of the reward APY is assumed lost
Yield
simple, not compounded; the APY is assumed to stay constant
Transactions
2 to enter, 1 to exit, and 1 claim per started 30 days when the pool pays rewards
Swaps
none for a single-asset pool: you are assumed to hold its asset. A multi-asset pool (or one of unknown exposure) swaps 50% of the position on the way in and back on the way out, at 0.30% (swap fee + slippage)
Impermanent loss
DeFiLlama's 7-day figure if there is one. Otherwise, for pools with two tokens, volatility^2 x T/365 / 8 of the position, from 30 days of daily prices (a 50/50 constant-product pool; a lower bound for concentrated liquidity). Capped at 50% of the position, and not counted when neither exists. Prices are looked up for every pool analysed, not only the pools shown.
TVL floor
pools under $100,000 are left out
Zero-APY pools
left out: they advertise nothing, so they mislead nobody (--include-zero-apy)

Limitations

  • These numbers come from a simple model. They are estimates, not predictions, and not financial advice.
  • The pool data comes from DeFiLlama and is only as good as what protocols and DeFiLlama report. APYs change every day.
  • Impermanent loss is estimated, not measured. DeFiLlama's own 7-day figure (il7d) was empty for every pool when this tool was written, so for pools with IL risk and exactly two tokens it is estimated from 30 days of daily prices: volatility squared times T/365 over 8, the small-move result for a 50/50 constant-product pool. For concentrated-liquidity pools (Uniswap v3 and v4, Velodrome v3 and similar) it is a lower bound. Pools with another number of tokens, or without prices, stay IL_UNKNOWN and their net APY is optimistic.
  • Prices are looked up for every pool analysed, not only the pools shown, so the counts and the order do not depend on the limit. A pool whose tokens have no usable prices, or whose prices could not be fetched, stays IL_UNKNOWN: no loss is counted for it, so the cost-negative counts are lower bounds for such pools.
  • Gas and L1 data fees are rough estimates. Swap cost is a flat rate, not a price-impact model, and a single-asset pool is assumed to need no swap because you already hold its asset: if you must buy it first, that swap is not counted.
  • The reward haircut is a rule of thumb, not a forecast of any token's price.
  • Smart contract risk, depegs, taxes, bridge costs and withdrawal limits are not modelled.
